Prototype sitemap

7 menu items. Daily work happens in the first four; the rest is occasional admin. Suggested walkthrough: Today → Calendar → Customer → Quick consult → Treatment record → Cash desk.

Daily

  • Sign in → My day
    Tap your name and enter a PIN. Specialists land on "My day" (short menu, big buttons); owner and reception land on Today. Tap your initials top right to switch user.
  • Today
    Today's appointments, alerts, AI digest · tab Reports: key numbers and Excel export
  • Calendar
    Click a slot to book or an appointment for details
  • Customers → profile
    Everything about one customer: timeline, health & consents, treatments, photos, packages, sales, account & payments
  • Quick consult (header button)
    Tap the customer's needs, get products and services with what to say; works for walk-ins
  • Treatment record
    Opened from an appointment: laser settings, shots, products used, photos
  • Cash desk
    Tabs: New sale (incl. pay later and package sale) · Sales (refunds) · Receivables · Expenses · Day close

Manage

  • Catalog & stock
    Tabs: Services · Products · Packages · Stock movements. Details: service, product
  • Team
    Employees and their activity; Commission tab (off by default). Detail: employee
  • Settings
    Business, hours, tax & receipts, features, AI, consent forms, integrations, audit log, users & roles, devices & rooms, SMS
  • Ask your data
    Sparkle icon in the header (Phase 2)
